Soil

Forms over hundreds to thousands of years

Soil is alive — full of minerals, water, air, and countless organisms that keep plants growing and ecosystems running.

04

Recent Past

~11,700 years ago and later

Each layer of earth is like a page in a history book, holding clues about the climate and life of the time it formed.

05

Ice Age

2.6 million – 11,700 years ago

Vast ice sheets covered much of the planet, shaping landscapes and challenging the creatures that lived through it.

06

Mesozoic Era

252 – 66 million years ago

The age of dinosaurs, when giant reptiles ruled the land, sea, and sky.

07

Paleozoic Era

541 – 252 million years ago

Life exploded in diversity — fish, insects, and the first creatures to crawl onto land all appeared in this era.

08

Precambrian

4.6 billion – 541 million years ago

Earth's earliest chapter — simple single-celled life slowly gave rise to the first complex organisms.

09

The Mantle

~35 – 2,890 km deep

Below the crust lies a thick layer of hot, slowly flowing rock — its movement drives volcanoes, earthquakes, and continents.

10

Earth's Core

~2,890 – 6,371 km deep

A molten outer core surrounds a solid inner core of iron and nickel, as hot as the surface of the sun.
